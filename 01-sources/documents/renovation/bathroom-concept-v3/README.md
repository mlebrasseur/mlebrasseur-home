# Basement Bathroom — Concept v3

Separate comparison concept started November 5, 2026; verification completed November 6: floating 36-inch Madison vanity with VT-SQ3620 top and matching LW1614-84GWH wall linen. Rear-left linen has rear/right hinges and a 5-inch rear-wall gap; vanity is centred between hamper and linen with about 5.10 inches of clear space on each side (November 6 revision). Hamper remains front-left. Sink top is modeled at 34 inches; cabinet bottoms align about 12.35 inches above the floor. Latest November 6 mirror revision: ALEX collection / BIBO 84-BIBO-24, 600 × 700 mm (nominal 24 × 28), centred above sink. Top aligns with linen at 67.46 inches; bottom 39.91 inches, about 5.91 inches above countertop. Two inset vertical light strips replace the earlier HIHI trial. Mounting positions are assumptions.

## Fit and Limits

The simplified 0–90° panel-envelope checks clear the modeled entrance door, linen doors and vanity door. Illustrative 14-inch drawer extension also clears tested furniture/shower envelopes; manufacturer slide travel was not established. The 5-inch linen offset resolves v2's modeled rear-wall/window/towel collision. The mirror bottom is 0.24 inch below the modeled faucet-lever top, with only 0.18 inch static depth separation between glass and lever. Actual faucet dimensions and lever travel remain unverified. Actual hinges, site dimensions, wall anchoring, plumbing and mounting positions remain unverified. Cabinet/basin details are schematic where drawings provide no dimensions.

Earlier [current concept](../bathroom-concept/README.md) and [v2](../bathroom-concept-v2/README.md) are preserved. V3 is a comparison, not a purchase or final construction decision.
