## Model Placement and Limits

The following placement records describe the product-dimension revision. **Later same-day update:** linen moved 2 inches from the front wall; hamper and hand-towel fixture recentered. See [current model assumptions](2026-11-05-bachelor-bathroom-model.md#provisional-dimensions-and-practical-limits) and [homeowner direction](../../chat/2026/2026-11-05-renovation-bathroom-model-corrections.md#further-november-5-revision--linen-clearance-and-white-vanity-base). Product dimensions below remain applicable.

- Vanity cabinet and top both meet the left and rear walls. The extra top width/depth projects toward the open sides; this is the homeowner's requested corner placement, not a verified installation detail. Body spans Z=39.492–86.5 in; top spans Z=38.665–86.5, X=0–18.307. Mirror is centred on the top at Z=62.583; outlet centred over its short end at X=9.154. Hamper position unchanged, leaving approximately 1.79 inches to the top and 1.625 inches to linen.
- Shower base occupies X=53–89, Z=0–47.75 in. Door/side glazing sits inboard, using the component spans above. Requested opening remains at the rear half of the long side when facing the shower from the vanity. The layout is not an installation template.
- End drain is provisionally at X=70.625, Z=8.375, under the removable stool. Base can be reversed; actual drain rough-in is unknown. This replaces the earlier central-drain assumption and its obsolete 6.9-inch stool/drain clearance claim. Feet sit outside the modeled cover; moving the stool is required for access. Pan elevation is illustratively 0.75 in, stool seat 17.25 in above room floor.
- Toledo stays centred at Z=71 with rear X=81; overall front X=54.2. Actual rotated flange position and behind-tank access remain unresolved.
- Door trim now has two recessed lines, mitered corners and illustrative satin white-painted wood grain. This is a requested visual treatment, not a selected moulding SKU. Door cut height remains unknown; stock 80-inch leaf stays in the model.
- Square shower faucet and black sink faucet shapes remain schematic; this revision establishes the shower enclosure/base, cabinet/top and provisional toilet dimensions, not valve or faucet installation dimensions.
