## Contract Terms (Document Summary)

- Page 1, articles 2–3: disbursement is conditional on down payment, satisfactory notary report and lender conditions. Accrued interest thirty days before the first monthly payment is payable at that date; successive disbursements may have interest deducted or collected separately.
- Page 1, articles 4–5: payments go first to interest and applicable loan-insurance costs, then principal. Unpaid interest can be capitalized. Article 4.1 permits a demand for the excess and revised repayment terms when principal, accrued interest and other amounts exceed the initial loan plus 5% of the lender-accepted property value. That value is not supplied here; no trigger threshold is calculated.
- Page 2, article 6: conversion to a closed fixed rate for the remaining term uses the then-recommended term immediately above the remaining duration, subject to a new agreement; a longer term may be authorized. Read with the separate conversion-discount agreement (SRC-2026-041).
- Page 2, article 7: payments may increase once per calendar year, up to double the original payment over the term, and may subsequently reduce to the original amount.
- Page 2, article 8.1: partial prepayments of at least 100 CAD, up to 15% of initial principal per calendar year (76,800 CAD calculated), without indemnity. Unused allowance does not carry forward. Regular payments continue. Excess partial prepayments incur three months' interest on the excess. Full repayment has separate rules.
- Page 2, article 8.2: full prepayment generally incurs three months' interest on the repaid balance without deducting the annual allowance; less than three months remaining uses the remaining duration. Article 8.1 includes an exception where the balance is within the penalty-free allowance.
- Pages 2–3, article 8.3: proportional cashback repayment applies if cashback was granted and prepayment indemnity is owed. No actual cashback receipt is established by the example.
- Page 3, articles 9–10: default and acceleration provisions are retained in the original; no default is reported.
- Page 4, article 13: lender may obtain mortgage insurance at its own expense and collect/share necessary information. This is distinct from the declined life/disability coverage in SRC-2026-041.
