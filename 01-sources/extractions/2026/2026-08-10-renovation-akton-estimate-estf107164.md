## Written Repair Scope for Each Crack

- Protect the nearby ground with plywood.
- Excavate an appropriately sized opening to the footing or 16 inches below the floor.
- Clean the wall with a wire brush.
- Repair by below-grade polyurethane injection beginning one foot above grade:
  - drill holes at approximately six-inch intervals and a 45-degree angle;
  - install injection fittings;
  - flush the crack with mild acid and then water;
  - cover the crack with hydraulic cement;
  - inject polyurethane at 3,000 to 10,000 psi;
  - remove the fittings and fill the holes with hydraulic cement.
- Cover the repair with Aqua-Bloc 770-06 liquid elastomeric membrane, Blueskin WP200 self-adhesive rubberized membrane and Delta-MS drainage/protection panel.
- Backfill with the excavated soil. The estimate warns that the backfilled soil will initially remain above grade while it settles.
- Clean the work area.

## Warranty and Exclusions

- Written warranty: 10 years, fully transferable.
- Coverage is limited to water infiltration through the repaired exterior crack.
- The warranty does not cover soil movement, parging, the wall as a whole, consequential water damage or landscaping after the repair.
- Akton does not restore landscaping.
- Additional charges apply if cement, asphalt or large rocks must be broken or removed below grade.
- The general terms place permit and licence costs on the client where required.
- Akton limits responsibility for undisclosed underground conduits, pipes and wiring; the terms refer to Info-Excavation information.
- Changes and additions require written consent from both parties.
- The client must provide access to water, electricity, equipment and connections needed for the work.
- The terms authorize Akton signs on the property for up to seven days after completion.
- Written permission from an adjacent owner is required before work encroaches on neighbouring property.

## Payment Terms
